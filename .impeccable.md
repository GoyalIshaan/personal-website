## Design Context

### Users

The primary audience is AI-infrastructure and software-engineering recruiters, hiring managers, and technical founders reviewing Ishaan's work quickly. They need to understand his current role, systems depth, measurable impact, and strongest projects without decoding resume jargon or hunting across pages.

### Brand Personality

Precise, understated, and technically ambitious. The voice should feel like a builder explaining real systems: specific about constraints and outcomes, confident without hype, and approachable enough to invite a technical conversation.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the site's original compact MagicUI portfolio language: the friendly "Hi, I'm Ishaan" hero, avatar, blur-fade entrances, expandable resume rows, image-backed project cards, technology badges, category filters, light/dark modes, and magnifying bottom dock. New content should fit those established components rather than replacing them with a new editorial or text-only system.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with current, verifiable work and measurable outcomes from the supplied resumes.
2. Make the first scan useful: role, specialization, education, and strongest systems projects should be obvious within seconds.
3. Treat the existing widgets and interactions as product identity; update their content instead of redesigning them.
4. Retain prior projects and experience when adding newer resume material unless the creator explicitly asks to remove something.
5. Keep one shared project source for home and project views so claims, dates, and descriptions do not drift.
